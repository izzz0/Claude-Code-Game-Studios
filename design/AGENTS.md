# Game design

The minimal design record is design/game-brief.md: pitch, core loop, player goal
and fail state, MVP, exclusions, and build order. Keep it short and testable.
Use $ccgs-brainstorm for design and $ccgs-create-stories for implementation scope.
Preserve existing design documents and approved decisions. Do not require the
unported standard/full GDD workflow or invent approval on a user's behalf.
